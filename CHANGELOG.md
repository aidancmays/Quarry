# Changelog

## 0.2.0

- Spawn Activation: picks which hand-placed spawn points a level uses from its seed and difficulty (more when
  harder, groups that tend to come together, more so when harder; Always and Min Difficulty points, spacing, clear
  of the start, a cap).
- Wave Plan: how many waves, how many in each (growing) and the pause between them, by difficulty.

## 0.1.0

- Health (server-authoritative, Dead tag), Damage Info, Knockback and Knockback Profile (damage and damage type
  push and stagger, over Poise; Rigidbodies and self-moving movers), Health Bar (screen overlay, shows when hurt,
  fades), Senses and INoiseSource, and the optional Cleave adapter (Cleave Damage).
